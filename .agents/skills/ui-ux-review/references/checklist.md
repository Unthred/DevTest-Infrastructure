# Review checklist

Ask these per page or task. Record only real problems.

## Purpose and hierarchy
- Is the page's purpose clear within five seconds?
- Is the most important status or action seen first?
- Does the content earn its position, or does history push out what needs
  attention?

## Content
- What can be removed, merged or moved behind a drill-down?
- Is anything repeated in the same view?
- Is any status meaningless without a next action?

## Drill-down
- Does each summary lead straight to its detail?
- Does the detail add something, or repeat the summary?
- Can you get back without losing your place?
- Is detail hidden on the summary page, or summary missing from a long
  detail page?

## Wording
- Does it use the user's words, not internal terms?
- Does one thing have one name everywhere?
- Do buttons say what happens?
- Do errors say what happened and what to do?
- Do empty states explain why and what next?
- Does truncation hide the part that tells items apart?

## Controls and consistency
- Do the same controls look and act the same on every page?
- Are clickable things obvious, and non-clickable things not?
- Is current selection shown correctly, and does it match the URL and
  data?
- Are destructive or irreversible actions distinct?

## Navigation and steps
- Can the main tasks be done in the fewest sensible clicks?
- Are there dead ends?
- Does Back preserve filters and position?
- Do links open what their label promises?

## Layout
- How much of the first screen is chrome rather than content?
- Is there wasted space, or unnecessary scrolling?
- Does width stay stable between adjacent pages?
- Are alignment and spacing consistent?

## Phone
- Is it designed for phone rather than shrunk?
- Are main tasks reachable with one hand, without sideways scrolling?

## Accessibility: automated checks are only a floor
- axe has no WCAG 2.1 AA violations.
- Keyboard: order is logical, focus is visible, and nothing traps focus.
- Status is not shown by colour alone.
- Contrast is correct in both themes.
- Motion respects reduced-motion settings.

## Aesthetic: report last
- Type scale, colour tokens, icon style and elevation are used
  consistently.
- The page looks deliberate and polished without losing its identity.
